Delete your account

You can permanently delete your account at any time directly in the app: go to Profile → Delete Account, confirm your password, and confirm the deletion. This immediately and permanently removes your account, profile, and posts, and cannot be undone.

If you're unable to access the app, you can instead email andrew@pixielabs.io with the subject line "Delete my account" and your in-app username, and your account and data will be removed within 7 days.
