Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

AM Tracker ("the app") is operated by Pixie Labs (UK) Ltd ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password, used to create and sign in to your account via Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile information: your display name and, optionally, a profile photo you choose to upload.
  • Content you create: workout logs, food logs, posts, comments, and upvotes that you add within the app, including any photos you attach to them.

How we use this information

Information you provide is used solely to operate the app's core features: authenticating your account, displaying your profile and content to other users you share it with, and syncing your data across your devices. We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore), a third-party cloud service. Photos you upload are stored inline within our Firestore database, not with a separate third-party media host. Firebase's own privacy practices are described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Analytics and advertising

We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in this app.

Data sharing with other users

Content you post (such as posts, comments, and workout/food logs you choose to share) may be visible to other users of the app, such as friends, family, or community members you're connected with, depending on the app's sharing features.

Your choices

You can edit or delete content you've created directly within the app. To delete your account and associated data entirely, see our Support page.

Children's privacy

AM Tracker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to andrew@pixielabs.io.
